## Επιστημονικές αποφάσεις
- Robust-MDP sample complexity is already sufficiently supported by one comprehensive canonical source spanning divergence family, radius and rectangularity.
- Generative-model access is a separate information budget and must not be compared as ordinary online interaction.
- Reward-proxy robustness/reward hacking remains separate from exogenous reward non-stationarity.
- Robust MARL equilibrium problems are not evidence for single-agent environmental-change resilience.
- Fault-tolerant control recovery can be useful external-validity inspiration without entering the core evidence tier.
- Reviews, seminars, publication webpages and standards indexes do not count independently when the primary scientific result is already selected.
