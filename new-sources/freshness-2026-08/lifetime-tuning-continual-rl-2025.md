# Position: Lifetime tuning is incompatible with continual reinforcement learning

Authors: Golnaz Mesbahi; Parham Mohammad Panahi; Olya Mastikhina; Steven Tang; Martha White; Adam White
Year: 2025
Venue: Proceedings of the 42nd International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML), PMLR 267
Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v267/main/assets/mesbahi25a/mesbahi25a.pdf
Landing page: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/mesbahi25a.html

## Intake note

This peer-reviewed position paper studies evaluation methodology for continual reinforcement learning and argues that tuning hyperparameters using access to the agent's full deployment lifetime can invalidate the intended continual-learning comparison. Its empirical analysis uses continuing and non-stationary environments to show that unrestricted lifetime tuning can obscure differences between continual and standard methods.

Potential thesis relevance: it may materially affect the tuning-data boundary, pilot/final separation, and fairness protocol for online-adaptive agents. The scientific review should determine which recommendations transfer to the bounded GridWorld experiment and how they interact with the existing statistical-evaluation evidence.
