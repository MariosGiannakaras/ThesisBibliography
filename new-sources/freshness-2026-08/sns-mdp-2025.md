## Intake note

This paper studies a structured form of non-stationarity in which the environment switches among MDPs according to an underlying Markov chain. It derives a value formulation for the switching process and analyzes TD learning, policy improvement, and Q-learning under the stated switching assumptions.

Potential thesis relevance: it may materially affect how a plain Q-learning baseline is interpreted in structured switching environments. It should be checked against the existing non-stationary/context-switching corpus before any claim that ordinary Q-learning is intrinsically incapable of handling all forms of non-stationarity.
