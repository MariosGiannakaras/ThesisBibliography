Year: 2025
Venue: Proceedings of The 28th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Statistics (AISTATS), PMLR 258

## Intake note

This peer-reviewed paper examines practical limitations of prior-free black-box non-stationary RL detection using MASTER and compares its behavior with random-restart and quickest-change-detection restart approaches in a piecewise-stationary setting.

Potential thesis relevance: it can constrain claims about generic change detection and may justify a simple detector/restart diagnostic baseline or, at minimum, a stronger requirement to validate detector behavior at realistic horizons instead of assuming theoretical regret guarantees imply practical detection.

The scientific review should distinguish results obtained in piecewise-stationary multi-armed bandits from claims that can legitimately transfer to a finite GridWorld MDP.
